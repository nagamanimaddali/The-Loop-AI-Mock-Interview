about the loop

A live AI interviewer that runs the loop with you.

The Loop is a practice ground for technical interviews — DSA, CS fundamentals, and language-specific rounds — with an AI interviewer that asks real follow-up questions instead of reciting a script.

questions
FAQ
Is this a real interviewer or a script? +
Neither, exactly — it's an AI model that generates and adapts questions live based on your answers, so no two sessions play out the same way.
Do I need to type my answers, or can I speak? +
Sessions are text-based for now — you type your answers, just like a chat conversation.
How is my score calculated? +
Each answer is evaluated for technical correctness, communication clarity, and problem-solving approach, then combined into an overall score and hiring recommendation.
Can I revisit past interviews? +
Yes — every completed session is saved to your history with its full report and score.
